# v0.4.0b6 - Provisional ULO Fuel Control

Adds the agreed temporary policy, separate from cost advisory:

- Ultra-low tier: gas at <= 2 C, heat pump at > 4 C, otherwise retain source.
- All other tiers: gas.
- Card Automatic enables/disables the policy once Heat mode is confirmed.
- Outdoor sensor, heating support and Climado control must be configured/on.

Automatic handoffs use the existing cloud commands and fuel confirmation.
They wait for a completed heating cycle, 30 minutes of source dwell, confirmed
Off, and five continuous minutes of valid stopped-equipment observations before
starting the next source. Weather and tier are rechecked before starting.
Minimum device protection settings remain in force; fan settings are untouched.

No writes use HomeKit. Fresh local feedback, when configured, must agree with
Ecobee. Missing outdoor/mode/fuel observations block handoffs. Windows pause,
Climado disable and manual changes take precedence. A failed/interrupted handoff
or restart during a handoff disables Automatic and reports an alert without
replaying a command. Heating can remain Off and requires manual attention.

This is not validated cost optimization or an automatic low-capacity fallback.
No matched-coil performance values or prices are assumed by the controller.
The fixed rule does not change automatically with future tariff updates.

## Installation And Live Checks

Automatic is disabled by default. After installing:

1. Configure the intended outdoor temperature sensor; confirm Celsius and fresh
   reports. Confirm heating support, master control, cloud Heat and Aux state.
2. Enable Automatic; check the card's weather, intent and minimum-source wait.
3. Observe both handoffs at real qualifying boundaries with someone home.
   Never spoof live temperature/entity states to force equipment transitions.
4. Verify 23:00/07:00 tier boundaries, no source churn in the 2-4 C band,
   existing comfort profile recovery, and fan settings unchanged.
5. Check Windows pause and manual Off precedence. Use simulated tests for
   missing feedback, service/storage failures and interrupted/restart cases.
