# v0.4.0b7 - Electricity Plan And Energy Price

- Adds a persisted ULO / TOU selector to the card and Home Assistant.
- Displays the current published energy-only price in CAD cents/kWh.
- Updates the timeline and price at rate boundaries, holidays and seasonal changes.
- Applies the November 1, 2026 prices on their effective date, not before.
- Keeps price information visible when Climado control or HVAC is off.

The selector changes Climado's schedule, not your electricity supplier's billing
plan. Delivery, taxes and rebates are excluded from the displayed price. Dated
prices cover November 2025 through October 2027; outside that interval the price
is unavailable until updated.

TOU uses Ontario's summer/winter schedules and the configured workday sensor for
holidays. Existing custom ULO schedules remain saved. The read-only timeline
uses the current backend schedule rather than a cached editor draft.

Selecting TOU disables the provisional ULO fuel policy without switching the
current heating source. ULO heat-cost advice is hidden under TOU. Returning to
ULO does not reactivate Automatic. Plan changes are blocked during a source
handoff. This release does not add TOU automatic fuel optimization.
